Participants · 참가자·윤리

건강한 성인 남성 15명. 전체 기록의 평균±표본 SD는 나이 22.1±2.2세, 키 177.3±5.9 cm, 체중 77.3±8.7 kg입니다.

하지 수술·골절 병력, 하지 움직임의 제한·통증, 근육·힘줄·인대 손상 등의 근골격계 질환이 제외 기준입니다. 실제 윤리 승인·승인 식별자·서면 동의 절차는 저자 확인란에서 보완합니다.

Experimental design and procedures

2동작(Walking/Split)×2일치성(congruent/incongruent)×8이동방향×2반복으로 1인당 64시행입니다. 첫 동작은 무작위로 선택하고 두 동작을 교대로 실시했습니다. 동작별 32시행은 무작위로 섞어 16시행씩 나누었고, 네 블록 사이에 2분 휴식을 부여했습니다.

force plate 중심에서 8방향으로 2 m에 목표 마커를 두고 해당 마커를 최대한 빨리 지나가도록 지시했습니다. 두 인지 조건 모두 화살표 머리 방향으로 이동했습니다. Congruent는 제시 위치에서 바깥쪽, incongruent는 중심쪽을 가리키므로 제시 위치와 이동 방향이 반대입니다.

약 15분의 교육·적응에 congruent에서 각 동작 3분, 두 동작의 congruent/incongruent를 합쳐 3분 연습이 포함됐습니다. 처음 잘못 출발한 경우 멈추지 않고 올바른 목표로 수정해 이동하도록 지시했습니다.

Data collection · 수집

분석한 3차원 마커 기록의 샘플링은 100 Hz입니다. 골반 중심은 RIC, LIC, RASIS, LASIS, RPSIS, LPSIS를 사용했습니다. 실제 장비·카메라·보정·좌표·선행 필터/gap filling·신호 동기화 세부는 확인란에 남아 있습니다. EMG/MVC 준비 사실과 분석에 사용한 운동학 자료를 구분합니다.

Data processing · 지표와 가용성

저장된 초록 신호 시각을 가장 가까운 마커 프레임에 맞추고, 그 프레임의 골반 중심 위치에서 수평 변위가 처음 1 m 이상이 된 기록 샘플을 종료로 했습니다. Time to 1 m은 이 구간의 시간, path length는 인접 위치의 누적 거리, mean speed는 각 시행의 path/time입니다.

960기록 중 955기록이 1 m 역치에 도달했습니다. 15명×32조건의 480개 평균 중 475개는 2회, 5개는 1회 유효 시행에서 계산됐습니다. 두 유효 시행에서는 일부 프레임에 골반 마커 5개가 가용했으며, 가용 좌표 평균을 사용했습니다.

Supplementary Methods / Results · 추가 진단

After familiarization, EMG electrodes were attached and muscle-specific maximal voluntary contractions were performed, followed by reflective marker placement. The reported markers included RACR, LACR, C7, STRN, XPHOID, T10, RASIS, LASIS, RPSIS, LPSIS, REPI_L, LEPI_L, RMAL_L, LMAL_L, RHEEL, LHEEL, RTOE, LTOE, R5THMETA, L5THMETA, RIC, and LIC. EMG muscle selection, acquisition settings, and MVC procedures require confirmation before an EMG analysis is reported.

The present outcomes were calculated from the horizontal pelvic marker center. The analysis endpoint was the first recorded sample with a displacement of at least 1 m from the green-signal reference position; threshold crossing was not interpolated. The task targets were located 2 m from the force plate center. Horizontal path length and trialwise path/time were converted from millimeters and millimeters per second to meters and meters per second before reporting.

Of 960 records, 955 reached the threshold. There were 480 participant-level condition means per outcome: 475 were based on two valid trials and five on one. Two valid trials contained frames at which five rather than six pelvic markers were available. The available coordinates were averaged, as implemented in the original code. No missing condition mean was imputed. The physical reason for the five records without a sampled crossing was not established.

Supplementary Methods — retrospective diagnostics

Additional diagnostics were performed in Python 3.12.14 with SciPy 1.18.1. Shapiro–Wilk tests and Q–Q plots were applied to centered orthonormal contrast scores for each model effect and the eight direction-specific paired Walking–Split differences across the 15 participants. Diagnostic p values were interpreted descriptively at 0.05 without multiplicity adjustment. Participant influence on the movement condition × direction interaction was examined by repeating each ANOVA after omitting one participant at a time. These diagnostics were additional retrospective analyses, not procedures identified in the original MATLAB code, and did not replace the original primary inference.

Supplementary Results — diagnostics and sensitivity

Departures from normality were identified in 1 of 31 effect contrast components for time to 1 m, 8 of 31 for horizontal path length, and 2 of 31 for mean speed. Among the eight direction-specific paired movement differences, departures were detected in none for time to 1 m, five for horizontal path length (D1, D3, D6, D7, and D8), and three for mean speed (D6, D7, and D8). These tests did not establish multivariate normality.

In leave-one-participant-out analyses, the movement condition × direction interaction remained significant in all 15 analyses for time to 1 m and horizontal path length, and in 12 of 15 analyses for mean speed. For mean speed, Greenhouse–Geisser-adjusted p values ranged from 0.0127 to 0.0645 across all 15 omission analyses. This sensitivity does not resolve nonnormality or establish the justification for excluding a participant.

The separately prepared Holm sensitivity across eight direction-specific movement contrasts per outcome retained the time contrasts in D3 and D7, but not D4 and D8. Path-length contrasts in D5–D8 and mean-speed contrasts in all eight directions remained significant. These unchanged estimates and additionally adjusted p values did not replace the original within-direction comparison procedure.
