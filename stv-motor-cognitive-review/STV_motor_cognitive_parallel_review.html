A random-effects model meta-analysis (Fig. 6) on 59 comparisons from 21 studies revealed a significant positive effect of SVT on motor function (SMD = 0.477; SE = 0.083; 95% CI = 0.313 to 0.640; Z = 5.720; P < 0.001). A heterogeneity level across individual effect sizes was relatively low (I² = 25.3%), whereas an Egger’s regression test found a significant publication bias (β₀ = 2.840; P = 0.002).

A random-effects model meta-analysis (Fig. 16) on 23 comparisons from 12 studies revealed a significant positive effect of SVT on cognitive function (SMD = 0.770; SE = 0.171; 95% CI = 0.434 to 1.105; Z = 4.500; P < 0.001). A heterogeneity level across individual effect sizes was relatively moderate (I² = 43.7%), whereas an Egger’s regression test found a significant publication bias (β₀ = 5.424; P < 0.001).
